# Fuentes y créditos

1. OMS y UNICEF. *Early Adolescent Skills for Emotions (EASE): group psychological help for young adolescents affected by distress in communities exposed to adversity. Generic field-trial version 1.0, 2023.* Ginebra: OMS. ISBN 978-92-4-008275-5. Licencia CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO. 3. OMS. *Mental Health Gap Action Programme (mhGAP) guideline for mental, neurological and substance use disorders, 2023.* Ginebra: OMS. ISBN 978-92-4-008427-8. Licencia CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO.
